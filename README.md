# Superstore Regional Profitability Story

## Overview

This project is an interactive **Tableau data visualization and business
intelligence project** built using the Superstore dataset.

The project focuses on understanding **regional profitability** and
presenting business performance through an interactive Tableau story.

The main Tableau workbook is:

``` text
Superstore Regional Profitability Story.twbx
```

A `.twbx` (Tableau Packaged Workbook) contains the Tableau workbook
together with the resources required to work with the visualization.

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to use Tableau to explore Superstore
business performance and identify differences in **profitability across
regions**.

The analysis is designed to help answer questions such as:

-   Which regions perform most strongly in terms of profitability?
-   Which areas contribute positively or negatively to overall profit?
-   How does profitability vary geographically?
-   What patterns can be identified from regional sales and profit
    performance?
-   How can visual analytics support better business decisions?

## Dataset

The project uses the widely used **Superstore retail dataset**, which
contains transactional information about retail orders.

Typical dimensions and measures in the Superstore dataset include:

-   Order information
-   Customer information
-   Product information
-   Geographic information
-   Sales
-   Quantity
-   Discount
-   Profit
-   Category
-   Sub-Category
-   Segment
-   Region

The project focuses particularly on the relationship between
**geography, sales performance, and profitability**.

## Dashboard / Story

The Tableau workbook is structured as a **profitability story**,
allowing the user to explore the data visually rather than relying only
on static tables.

The visualization approach emphasizes:

### Regional Performance

Comparison of profitability across geographic regions to identify
stronger and weaker-performing areas.

### Sales and Profitability

Analysis of sales alongside profit to distinguish between regions that
generate high revenue and regions that generate high actual profit.

### Geographic Patterns

Use of geographic visualization and/or regional comparisons to make
differences in business performance easier to identify.

### Business Interpretation

The visual analysis is intended to translate raw transactional data into
business-oriented insights that can support decisions around regional
performance and profitability.

## Key Business Questions

The project can be used to investigate:

1.  Which regions are the most profitable?
2.  Which regions have comparatively weak profitability?
3.  Are high-sales regions necessarily the most profitable?
4.  Where are potential opportunities for improving profitability?
5.  Are there geographical patterns in the company's performance?

## How to Run

### Prerequisites

You will need **Tableau Desktop** or a compatible Tableau environment
capable of opening `.twbx` files.

### Steps

1.  Clone or download this repository.
2.  Open:

``` text
Superstore Regional Profitability Story.twbx
```

3.  The workbook should open in Tableau with the packaged resources
    available.
4.  Navigate through the Tableau story/dashboard to explore the regional
    profitability analysis.
5.  Interact with available filters, charts, and visualizations to
    investigate different aspects of the data.

## Business Value

Regional profitability analysis can help a business identify where
performance is strong and where improvement may be required.

Rather than looking only at total sales, profitability analysis provides
a more useful view of business performance by considering the value
generated after costs and discounts represented in the dataset.

The resulting visual story can therefore support decisions around:

-   Regional strategy
-   Resource allocation
-   Product and sales strategy
-   Profit improvement
-   Identification of underperforming markets

## Limitations

The analysis is based on the Superstore dataset and therefore reflects
the characteristics and limitations of that dataset.

The visualizations show patterns and relationships in the available data
but should not automatically be interpreted as evidence of causation.

Additional business information, such as operating costs, market
conditions, customer acquisition costs, and external economic factors,
could provide a more complete picture of regional profitability.

## Future Improvements

Possible extensions include:

-   Adding year-over-year profitability comparisons.
-   Including profit margin alongside absolute profit.
-   Analyzing profitability at the state and city levels.
-   Examining the effect of discounts on regional profit.
-   Adding customer-segment analysis.
-   Adding product-category and sub-category profitability.
-   Creating an executive KPI dashboard.
-   Publishing the Tableau story to Tableau Public for easier sharing.
-   Adding recommendations directly into the dashboard based on
    identified underperforming regions.
